Each selected stack is one physical row position. The FRONT section and optional BACK section are independent: product, facings, depth heights, package, and orientation can differ. Back depth heights are entered backside → inward. This allows Pepsi long-side from the front and Dew short-side from the back in the same physical row. 12-pack assets use *-12-long/short.png. 24-pack assets use *-24-long/short.png. Mixed rows split the front depth between two products. 6-pack shell assets use *-6pk-long/short.png. 6-pack shells are available for Pepsi, Diet Pepsi, Mtn Dew, Diet Mtn Dew, Dr Pepper, and Diet Dr Pepper.
